# Low-Cost Tech Business Ideas (Proven Abroad, Not Yet in Iran)

A working list of simple, technical (app/website) business models that have made real money for small teams or solo founders in the US and other first-world markets, but are not yet established in the Iranian market. Each idea is fast to build, cheap to start, and adaptable to Iran.

> **Key insight:** Because Big Tech (Google, Meta, Stripe, etc.) is legally barred from Iran, the market historically rewards well-built **local versions** of proven foreign products. Copying a validated Western model and localizing it (Persian language, Rial payments, local app stores) is a proven strategy, not a stigma.
> Source: [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/803944) (content rephrased for compliance).

---

## How to read the cost estimates

- **DIY (you build it):** main costs are a domain (~$10/year) and hosting (~$5-20/month, cheaper on local Iranian hosts paid in Rial). Labor is your own time.
- **Hire a developer:** add a one-time build cost. In Iran this is far cheaper than the US; ranges below are rough.
- These are **bootstrapped micro-businesses** by design: low risk, fast to test, no investors needed.

## 1. Niche Directory Website

**How it works:** A searchable, curated list of supply in one narrow niche (e.g., tools, professionals, services). Visitors come for the curated list; you charge the listed businesses for visibility.

**How it makes money:** Paid/featured listings, banner ads, lead generation (selling qualified inquiries), and premium subscriptions for enhanced profiles.

**Technical requirements:** A simple website with a database of listings, search/filter, and a payment page. Can be built with no-code tools or a basic web stack. No mobile app needed.

**Target customers:** The businesses being listed (they pay for placement) + a free audience of searchers who create the demand.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$50-150 (domain + hosting + a directory template). Hire a dev ~$300-1,500.

**Time to build:** A few hours to 2 weeks. (Cursor Directory was reportedly built in a few hours.)

**Proven example:** Cursor Directory ~$35K/month at ~99.8% margin ([stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/how-to-build-monetize-profitable-web-directory-2025)); another founder's directory hit ~$35K/month after going viral ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/video/website-makes-35k-month-built-050007670.html)); Spotcrime ~$79K/month from a simple search interface ([directoryeasy.com](https://directoryeasy.com/blog/how-to-build-a-profitable-directory-website)). General model: [eDirectory](https://www.edirectory.com/updates/profitable-directory-website-ideas-2026/).

**Iran localization:** Persian-language directories are underserved. Examples: vetted contractors, pediatric dentists, English/IELTS tutors, immigration lawyers, pet services, auto mechanics. Charge for featured placement in Rial via a local gateway.

## 2. Social Media Scheduling / Content Repurposing Tool

**How it works:** Users connect their social accounts, write posts once, and the tool schedules and auto-publishes them across platforms (or repurposes one format into another).

**How it makes money:** Monthly subscription (SaaS), usually tiered by number of accounts/posts.

**Technical requirements:** Account integrations (APIs), a scheduling/queue backend, a calendar UI. More technical than a directory but still a solo-buildable web app.

**Target customers:** Small businesses, content creators, marketing agencies, social media managers.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$100-300 (hosting + API costs). Hire a dev ~$1,000-4,000.

**Time to build:** 2-6 weeks for an MVP.

**Proven example:** Post Bridge ~$35K MRR ~18 months after launch ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/hitting-35k-mrr-after-struggling-to-make-money-online-for-four-years-CNVpxrwIVqxcsfZ4fERw)); BlogToPin (blog-to-Pinterest repurposing) ~$15K MRR ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/hitting-16k-mrr-after-years-of-failed-products-h5lM0xJmVrRZM0B23MT6)).

**Iran localization:** Instagram and Telegram dominate. Build a scheduler that cross-posts to **Instagram + Telegram channels**, with Persian captions and **Jalali (Shamsi) calendar** scheduling. No polished local competitor exists.

## 3. Link-in-Bio + Mini Storefront for Instagram Sellers

**How it works:** A single shareable page (the "link in bio") that holds a seller's product catalog and links. Add a simple checkout so buyers can order/pay without manual DMs.

**How it makes money:** Freemium subscription for the page + premium features (custom domain, analytics), or a small fee per transaction.

**Technical requirements:** A page builder, product catalog, and a local payment integration. Relatively simple web app.

**Target customers:** Instagram shops and creators (Iran has hundreds of thousands of these).

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$100-300. Hire a dev ~$1,000-3,000.

**Time to build:** 2-4 weeks.

**Proven example:** Linktree grew from this exact simple idea to a major company ([Substack research](https://mclauchlan.substack.com/p/i-researched-50-successful-micro)).

**Iran localization:** Instagram is the #1 e-commerce channel in Iran, but checkout is still manual (DM + card-to-card). A link-in-bio page with catalog + **Zarinpal/IDPay** checkout solves a real daily pain.

## 4. QR-Code Digital Menu + Ordering for Cafés/Restaurants

**How it works:** Each table gets a QR code; scanning opens a digital menu. Optional add-ons: "call waiter," place an order, and pay.

**How it makes money:** Low monthly subscription per venue (SaaS), with tiers for ordering/payment features.

**Technical requirements:** A mobile-friendly web page (no app install needed), a menu management dashboard for the venue, QR generation. Very simple.

**Target customers:** Cafés, restaurants, fast-food spots, hookah lounges.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$50-200. Hire a dev ~$500-2,000.

**Time to build:** Days to 2 weeks.

**Proven example:** A B2B model built on a phone number + countertop QR code was described as a ~$5K/month side business ([Substack](https://zackliu.substack.com/p/how-to-turn-a-single-twilio-number)). QR menus are standard across the West.

**Iran localization:** Most Iranian cafés still use paper menus. Sell a QR menu + "call waiter" + order pad as a cheap monthly subscription. Easy to demo and close in person, venue by venue.

## 5. Niche Booking / Reservation System

**How it works:** Online booking for one specific vertical: pick a slot, book, get a reminder. Replaces phone/DM scheduling.

**How it makes money:** Monthly subscription per business, or a small fee per booking.

**Technical requirements:** Calendar/availability engine, booking flow, SMS/notification reminders, admin dashboard. Moderate complexity.

**Target customers:** Salons/barbershops, dental and beauty clinics, futsal/turf fields, and short-term villa rentals.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$150-400. Hire a dev ~$1,500-5,000.

**Time to build:** 3-6 weeks.

**Proven example:** Booking software for unglamorous niches (campgrounds, salons, clinics) is a known, durable money-maker ([redwerk](https://redwerk.com/blog/micro-saas-ideas-that-print-money/)).

**Iran localization:** Pick one vertical and own it. Strong opportunity: **short-term villa rentals in northern Iran** (a huge, mostly informal market), plus salons and futsal fields. Use SMS reminders (very effective in Iran).

## 6. AI Photo / Headshot App

**How it works:** User uploads a few selfies or product photos; the app generates professional headshots, profile pictures, or clean product shots using AI image models.

**How it makes money:** Pay-per-generation (credits) or a one-time/subscription fee.

**Technical requirements:** Front-end + image-generation model access (API or self-hosted/open-source model). **Note the Iran API constraint below.**

**Target customers:** Job seekers, professionals, and especially Instagram sellers needing clean product photos.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$200-600 (mostly model/compute costs). Hire a dev ~$2,000-6,000.

**Time to build:** 2-5 weeks.

**Proven example:** HeadshotPro reached ~$3.6M ARR built solo ([alexcloudstar](https://www.alexcloudstar.com/blog/one-person-startup-scaling-2026)); a hobby app turned into ~$25K/month ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/video/turned-hobby-25k-month-app-020005200.html)).

**Iran localization:** Big demand among Instagram sellers, priced in Rial. **Catch:** OpenAI and most US AI APIs block Iranian IPs, so you need a routing/proxy setup or local/open-source models. Treat this as a real engineering task.

## 7. Niche Job Board / Aggregator

**How it works:** Scrape or collect job listings for one specific niche into a single clean site. Optionally add a talent pool.

**How it makes money:** Featured/paid job posts, employer subscriptions, and (later) candidate placement fees.

**Technical requirements:** A scraper/aggregator, a listings database, and a clean front-end. Low-to-moderate complexity.

**Target customers:** Employers in the niche (they pay) + job seekers (free traffic).

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$50-200. Hire a dev ~$500-2,500.

**Time to build:** 1-3 weeks.

**Proven example:** A solo founder aggregated data-annotation listings and reached ~$1K/mo in 6 months ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/from-ai-job-board-to-talent-pool-and-hitting-1k-mo-in-6-months-cc99c09d89)).

**Iran localization:** Niche boards for remote/dollar-paid work, or specific sectors (medical, oil & gas, programming, design).

## 8. Wedding / Event Mini-Site Builder

**How it works:** A templated personal website for an event: RSVP, schedule, photo gallery, map, and gift/contribution links.

**How it makes money:** One-time fee per site or premium templates/features.

**Technical requirements:** Templated site builder + RSVP form + simple hosting. Simple.

**Target customers:** Couples getting married, event hosts, party planners.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$50-200. Hire a dev ~$500-2,000.

**Time to build:** 1-3 weeks.

**Proven example:** A large US category (Zola, The Knot) with essentially no equivalent in Iran.

**Iran localization:** Persian templates, Jalali dates, and RSVP via SMS. Pair with the booking idea (#5) for event venues.

## Iran-Specific Constraints (read before building)

These are exactly why foreign versions have not arrived, and why a localized build can win:

- **Payments:** Stripe/PayPal do not work. Use local gateways (**Zarinpal, IDPay, NextPay**) settling in Rial through **Shaparak**. Build payment in from day one.
- **Distribution:** Google Play billing and the App Store are unreliable/blocked. Publish on local Android stores (**Cafe Bazaar, Myket**) and ship a **PWA/web app**.
- **AI features:** OpenAI and most US APIs block Iranian IPs. AI ideas need a routing/proxy setup or local/open-source models. ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202605256845) notes rising filtering and access costs.)
- **Internet instability/filtering:** Keep apps lightweight and resilient to flaky connections.

## Where to Start & How to Validate Cheaply

**Best first picks** (fastest to money, lowest cost, no AI/API dependency): **#1 Niche Directory**, **#3 Link-in-Bio + checkout**, **#4 QR Menu**. All are buildable in days to ~2 weeks, monetize in Rial immediately, and have clear daily-pain customers.

**Cheapest validation:** Pick one niche, make a single landing page describing the product, and try to **pre-sell 5 paying businesses** (cafés, salons, Instagram shops). If 5 people pay a small monthly fee on a promise, the idea is real. If not, switch niches, the build is cheap enough that iterating is the whole game.

# Part 2 — More Business Ideas

## 9. Browser (Chrome) Extension

**How it works:** A small browser add-on that solves one specific annoyance (summarize YouTube videos, supercharge ChatGPT, block distractions, scrape data). Users install it from the Chrome Web Store.

**How it makes money:** Freemium with a paid tier, subscription, or one-time license via a third-party billing tool (Google shut down native Web Store payments, so use **ExtensionPay** or similar).

**Technical requirements:** HTML/CSS/JavaScript and the browser extension API. One of the lowest technical bars; often a weekend build.

**Target customers:** Global internet users in a niche (productivity, students, marketers, developers). This one is naturally **global**, not Iran-only.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$30-150 (incl. the one-time $5 Chrome developer fee + billing tool). Hire a dev ~$300-2,000.

**Time to build:** A weekend to 2 weeks.

**Proven example:** Superpower ChatGPT ~$20K-30K MRR ([stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/build-profitable-ai-chrome-extension-blueprint)); a YouTube-summarizer extension reportedly ~$540K/year ([Medium](https://medium.com/@udaya_22564/how-to-build-a-500-000-year-chrome-extension-in-2026-be161269c11d)); a simple productivity extension ~$4,200/month from 8,000 paying users at $4.99 ([fungies.io](https://fungies.io/monetize-chrome-extension-2026/)).

**Iran localization:** Best treated as a **global** product earning foreign currency. The build is doable from anywhere; the challenge is **getting paid into Iran** (see "Getting Paid from Global Customers" below). Local angle: extensions tuned for Persian sites/workflows.

## 10. Notion Templates / Digital Products

**How it works:** Build a useful template once (planner, budget tracker, business dashboard, content calendar) and sell unlimited copies. Same model applies to presets, e-books, and spreadsheets.

**How it makes money:** One-time sales (often $19-79 each); create once, sell infinitely with near-zero per-unit cost.

**Technical requirements:** Almost none, no coding. You need the skill to design the template and a storefront/landing page.

**Target customers:** Students, freelancers, small businesses, productivity enthusiasts.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$0-100. The product itself is free to make; cost is just a storefront/domain.

**Time to build:** Days.

**Proven example:** Thomas Frank crossed ~$1M selling two Notion templates ([cartmango](https://cartmango.com/how-to-sell-notion-templates/)); creators report $500-$50,000+/month; average template sells $19-79 ([fungies.io](https://fungies.io/sell-notion-templates-online-make-money-2026/)).

**Iran localization:** Sell Persian-language templates and digital products (e.g., freelancer accounting sheets, exam planners) via a local gateway or a Telegram-based store. Zero inventory, pure margin.

## 11. Paid Newsletter / Paid Telegram Channel

**How it works:** Publish valuable, focused content on one topic on a regular cadence. Readers pay a monthly fee for access.

**How it makes money:** Paid subscriptions; later, sponsorships and your own digital products.

**Technical requirements:** Minimal, an email tool or a private channel. The "product" is your expertise and consistency.

**Target customers:** People hungry for an information edge (investing, crypto, tech, English learning, immigration, freelancing).

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$0-50/month for an email platform.

**Time to build:** Start in a day; the work is ongoing writing.

**Proven example:** A one-person newsletter reportedly made ~$100K in a year ([Substack](https://escapethecubicle.substack.com/p/how-my-simple-newsletter-made-100k)).

**Iran localization:** **Telegram is dominant in Iran** and perfect for this. Run a free public channel as the funnel and a paid private channel (access sold in Rial via a local gateway or a bot). Strong niches: dollar/gold/crypto analysis, IELTS, remote-work job leads.

## 12. Single-Purpose AI Tool ("AI Wrapper") Micro-SaaS

**How it works:** A simple interface that takes user input (text, image, PDF, audio), sends it to an AI model with a fixed instruction, and returns a useful result (e.g., analyze a nutrition label, generate captions, summarize contracts).

**How it makes money:** Credits or subscription. The value is in the focused use case and marketing, not heavy engineering.

**Technical requirements:** A front-end + AI model API (or a self-hosted/open-source model). Buildable fast, sometimes no-code. **Note the Iran API constraint.**

**Target customers:** A specific group with a recurring task (creators, students, recruiters, small businesses).

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$100-500 (mostly model/compute). Hire a dev ~$1,000-5,000.

**Time to build:** Days to ~3 weeks.

**Proven example:** A no-code AI tool built in 3 weeks reached ~$40K MRR ([beehiiv](https://jacks-newsletter-0adf2e.beehiiv.com/p/how-an-ai-wrapper-hit-40k-mrr-and-what-it-means-for-our-tools-126a)); an AI dating-coach app ~$190K MRR ([stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/chatgpt-wrapper-business-strategy-guide)); Castmagic ~$30K+ MRR in year one ([creem.io](https://www.creem.io/blog/ai-saas-ideas-making-money-2026)).

**Iran localization:** Big opportunity for **Persian-language** AI tools (content for Instagram shops, exam prep, legal/contract drafting). **Catch:** US AI APIs block Iranian IPs, so plan for a proxy/routing setup or local/open-source models from day one.

## 13. Productized Service (Design / Video / Dev Subscription)

**How it works:** Sell a service as a fixed-price "product" with a clear scope and recurring delivery, instead of custom quotes. Classic version: a flat monthly fee for unlimited design (or video, or development) requests, handled one at a time.

**How it makes money:** Flat monthly subscription per client.

**Technical requirements:** Almost none to start, a landing page plus a task board (Trello/Notion) and your skill. This is the **lowest-tech, fastest-to-launch** model here.

**Target customers:** Startups, SaaS companies, agencies, and busy small businesses that need ongoing creative/dev work.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$50-200 (landing page + tools). The main input is your skill/time.

**Time to build:** A day to launch the offer (DesignJoy's site was reportedly built in ~7 hours).

**Proven example:** DesignJoy ~$3.1M ARR run by one person ([stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/blog/the-3m-empire-built-in-7-hours)); the design-subscription model runs ~$5,000/month for unlimited requests ([stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/scaling-to-million-dollar-solopreneur-workflow)); "Baked" scaled to ~$160K MRR ([stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/productized-service-playbook-2026-twitter-starter-story)).

**Iran localization:** Two paths: (a) serve **Iranian businesses** in Rial (logo/social/video packages for the many Instagram brands), or (b) serve **global clients** to earn foreign currency (see payout note below). Productized social-media-content packages are a clear local gap.

## 14. Online Course / Membership in a High-Demand Skill

**How it works:** Package your expertise into a structured course or a recurring membership community. Sell access.

**How it makes money:** One-time course sales, cohort fees, or monthly membership.

**Technical requirements:** Low, recording tools + a hosting/checkout platform (or a local equivalent / private channel). No custom development needed.

**Target customers:** Learners in high-demand skills: English/IELTS, programming, design, digital marketing, immigration prep, trading.

**Startup cost:** DIY ~$50-300 (recording gear + platform).

**Time to build:** 1-4 weeks to record and launch a first course.

**Proven example:** Median course on one platform earns ~$198/month, with some courses selling $100K+; the global online-education market is projected at ~$221.7B in 2026 ([Podia](https://www.podia.com/articles/best-online-course-platforms)).

**Iran localization:** Massive, proven demand for English, programming, and immigration prep. Sell via Rial gateways, deliver via a private platform or Telegram. Differentiate by niche depth (e.g., "Python for accountants," "IELTS speaking only").

---

## Getting Paid from Global Customers (for the global-facing ideas)

Ideas #9, #12, and #13 can target worldwide customers and earn in foreign currency, but **receiving that money inside Iran is the hard part** due to sanctions (Stripe/PayPal/Payoneer generally do not serve Iran). Common workarounds people use: a trusted partner or company abroad, a foreign bank account, or crypto settlement. Treat the payout method as a core part of the plan, not an afterthought, and verify it is legal and workable before you build.
